# Focus-area review

I reviewed all 202 bill classifications and updated 11 records. The assignments are editorial categories, distinct from Congress.gov’s policy areas.

- Primary focus reflects the main policy subject; secondary focus identifies one distinct, material additional subject.
- A federal agency appearing in a bill does not by itself establish a government-AI-adoption focus.
- Frontier AI is reserved for advanced/state-of-the-art-model risks, not all AI national-security legislation.
- Companions receive consistent assignments when their relevant provisions match.
- An adjacent digital-policy bill remains labeled by its actual subject; classification does not decide tracker inclusion.

## Category definitions

**AI Economy, Competition & Innovation:** Competition, market access, commercial innovation, investment, and economic incentives related to AI or the broader digital economy.

**AI Regulation & Governance:** Cross-sector AI regulatory frameworks, liability, preemption, regulatory sandboxes, and public oversight or ethical governance.

**Children & Youth:** Protections, age assurance, parental controls, and AI or online services specifically directed at minors.

**Cybersecurity:** Security of systems, networks, AI agents and models; incident reporting, cyber workforce and cyber resilience.

**Data Centers, Energy & Infrastructure:** Data-center development, electricity and water demand, utility rates, grid capacity, and associated physical infrastructure.

**Digital Assets & Financial Regulation:** Digital-asset market structure, financial oversight of digital commodities, and central-bank digital-currency restrictions.

**Education, Workforce & Literacy:** AI literacy, education, training, employment, worker protections and workforce capacity, including relevant public-sector hiring.

**Fraud & Scams:** Consumer or financial deception, scam prevention, cybercrime schemes and related enforcement.

**Frontier AI Safety & Security:** Evaluation, control, security or systemic-risk governance of advanced or state-of-the-art AI systems; not every use of AI by an adversary.

**Government & Public-Sector AI:** Public-sector AI adoption, procurement, agency AI operations, and AI-specific federal administrative requirements.

**Intellectual Property:** Patents, copyright, digital-replica rights, training-data rights and protection of proprietary model capabilities.

**National Security & Defense:** AI or emerging-technology threats involving terrorism, foreign adversaries, defense applications, intelligence and national-security coordination.

**Online Speech & Platform Governance:** Government coercion of speech intermediaries, platform editorial independence and content-governance rules, including AI speech providers.

**Privacy & Data Governance:** Personal-data rights, data handling and consent, data security, public-data stewardship and interoperability.

**Quantum Technology:** Quantum research, commercialization, government deployment, coordination and migration to post-quantum cryptography.

**Sector-Specific AI:** AI applications or oversight in a defined industry such as financial services, health, weather or laboratory science.

**Semiconductors & Advanced Tech:** Semiconductor hardware, manufacturing, supply chains and other explicitly covered critical/emerging hardware technologies; wireless-spectrum policy has its own category.

**Standards, Testing & Research:** Technical standards, evaluation methods, research resources, testbeds and scientific research programs.

**Synthetic Media & Content:** Digital replicas, deepfakes, intimate visual depictions, provenance, labeling and other AI-related content protections; some bills also cover authentic content.

**Telecommunications & Connectivity:** Wireless connectivity, spectrum, Wi-Fi policy and international communications-technology coordination.

**Trade & Export Controls:** Export restrictions, licensing, international technology controls and related trade-policy enforcement or protections.

**Transparency & Accountability:** Disclosure, reporting, audits, impact assessments, explainability, whistleblowing and mechanisms for holding AI actors accountable.

The complete record-by-record review, including retained labels and source basis, is saved in `data/focus-area-review.json`. No bills were added or removed.
